First, the important distinction

This is a community website about Durham, Connecticut. It is not the Town of Durham, and it is not an official town channel.

If your question is about town government — permits, taxes, schools, the library, a town service — please contact the town directly using the verified links below. That is the fastest way to get a real answer.

Contacting this website

[NEEDS VERIFICATION — site contact method]

This site needs a real way for people to reach the team that runs it. Do not publish an invented email address or phone number. The project brief supplied none.

Before launch, decide one of the following:

  1. A monitored email address — e.g. an inbox the site owner actually reads. Then replace this block with it.
  2. No contact form at all — if no one will monitor an inbox, remove this section entirely and rely on the official town links above. A form that collects messages nobody reads is worse than no form.
  3. A GitHub-issues link — if the site is run from a public repository, corrections could be submitted as issues there instead.

What to contact this site about

  • A correction to any fact on this site, with the source for the correction
  • A business listing for a Durham business (listings are verified before they are added)
  • A broken link — these are fixed as quickly as we can

What to send to the town instead

  • Permit questions, tax questions, and town records
  • School enrollment and district matters
  • Library cards, hours, and programs
  • Anything where you need an official answer on the record
