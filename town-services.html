Getting around

Public Transit

River Valley Transit (RVT) — the Estuary Transit District, doing business as River Valley Transit, and formerly known as the Middlesex Area Transit District (MAT) — serves 16 municipalities across Middlesex County and parts of New London County. Its services include fixed-route, deviated fixed-route, trolleys, shuttles, taxis, and paratransit.

Dial-A-Ride in Durham

Dial-A-Ride shared-ride service is available within Durham, along with Chester, Clinton, Deep River, Essex, East Haddam, East Hampton, Haddam, Killingworth, Lyme, Middlefield, Middletown, Portland, Old Lyme, Old Saybrook, and Westbrook.
