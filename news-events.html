The Durham Fair

This one is real, not a sample — and it is the entry worth featuring.

The 106th Annual Durham Fair runs September 24–27, 2026 on the Durham Town Green and adjoining fairgrounds. It has been 100% volunteer-run since 1916 and is one of the largest volunteer-run agricultural fairs in North America. A modern fair can draw 200,000+ visitors with good weather.

  • Dates: September 24–27, 2026
  • Location: Durham Town Green and adjoining fairgrounds
  • Website: durhamfair.com ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  • Contact: Durham Agricultural Fair Association, Inc. · 24 Townhouse Road, Durham, CT 06422 · (860) 349-9495
