Local Businesses in Durham

Durham has a real Main Street economy — and this directory gathers the town's businesses in one place. Every listing below is checked against the Town of Durham Economic Development Commission's official business directory, the business's own website, or another named source. Addresses are real; where a business genuinely has no website, we say "No website listed" rather than guess a URL.

A note on what is not listed here

The research file also contains five "low-confidence" registry entries — commercial addresses from a business-entity registry (16 Main Street; 104 Commerce Circle; 9 Commerce Circle; 47 Meeting House Hill Road; 17 Guire Road). Their operating status was not confirmed, and the researcher explicitly marked them "DO NOT publish as a storefront without re-verification."

They are deliberately excluded from this page. A directory that lists closed or unconfirmed businesses is worse than a shorter one.
