Welcome to Durham, Connecticut

Durham is a small town in Middlesex County, Connecticut — a place with deep farming roots, a river running through its center, and one of the largest volunteer-run fairs in North America.

About the town

A quick introduction

Durham keeps the traditional New England town government its residents have used for generations. The town is led by a three-member Board of Selectmen, elected to four-year terms, one of whom serves as First Selectman.

The town shares Regional School District 13 with its neighbor, Middlefield, and is one of sixteen municipalities served by River Valley Transit.

Landmarks and heritage

What Durham is known for

The Durham Fair

Every September, Durham hosts the Durham Fair on the Town Green and the adjoining fairgrounds. It is one of the largest volunteer-run agricultural fairs in North America — and it has been run entirely by volunteers since 1916.

The 106th Annual Durham Fair is scheduled for September 24–27, 2026.

Read more about the Durham Fair · Visit durhamfair.com ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Historic Main Street and the Coginchaug River

Durham's historic character comes from its days as a farming village on the Coginchaug River. The river still runs through town, and Main Street remains the heart of the community — home to the town's market, pharmacy, shops, and businesses.

Millers Pond State Park

Millers Pond State Park offers an easy, flat trail that circles the lake, with a few rocky spots, and rock outcrops that extend into the pond and are used as fishing platforms.
